01 — Thesis & Opportunity

Build demand first.
Let evidence determine the infrastructure.

TRACE Foods Company Limited builds the operating and intelligence layer for Africa's food system, starting with fresh produce. Seasons by TRACE is the first owned demand node and customer-facing expression of that system.

Renewed thesis: 2026-10-03
Status: Core
Renewed thesis. TRACE is not a cold-storage company looking for throughput and Seasons is not the whole company. TRACE coordinates sourcing, quality, movement, inventory, allocation and fulfilment across retail and B2B. The lot-movement database records what happens. Infrastructure is added only when recurring product flow proves the need and economics.
The company in four connected parts
01 — DEMAND

Seasons creates trusted, concentrated demand.

A curated food market where fresh produce is the anchor, Nigerian food and homegrown brands create discovery, and convenience makes better food easier to use.

02 — OPERATING SYSTEM

One backend serves more than one channel.

The same sourcing, receiving, quality, inventory and fulfilment capability serves Seasons, recurring B2B customers and future nodes.

03 — ERP + INTELLIGENCE

Every commercial movement becomes usable data.

TRACE is already building the lot-movement database. By the planned July launch, the target is an ERP foundation that is approximately 60% built around the workflows most critical to live operations.

04 — EVIDENCE-LED ASSETS

Partners first; ownership follows utilisation.

Transport, storage and specialist capacity are contracted first where practical. TRACE owns an asset only when throughput, utilisation and superior economics are demonstrated.

The problem TRACE solves
FRAGMENTED SUPPLY

Good supply is difficult to identify and repeat.

Suppliers lack clear demand signals and buyers lack consistent standards. TRACE creates product specifications, supplier scorecards and recurring purchase signals.

UNCERTAIN MOVEMENT

Headline purchase price hides the true cost.

Damage, delays, poor handling and rejected product raise the effective cost of every saleable kilogram.

FRAGMENTED DEMAND

Households and businesses solve the same problem separately.

Seasons and B2B aggregate demand into a shared operating system, improving purchasing, routing and inventory decisions.

The economic equation
Farm-gate purchase + transport + handling + packaging + loss ÷ saleable kilograms = landed sellable cost.
TRACE wins by improving the complete outcome—not by assuming direct sourcing, refrigeration or removing intermediaries is automatically cheaper.
